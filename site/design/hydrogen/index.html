Overview

Route, conditions, reactions and the headline numbers.

Natural gas is desulphurised, mixed with steam and reformed over nickel catalyst in a fired tubular reformer. A high temperature shift converts most of the CO to more hydrogen, the gas is cooled and dried, and a pressure swing adsorption unit delivers 99.9%+ hydrogen. PSA tail gas fuels the reformer.
