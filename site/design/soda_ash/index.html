Overview

Route, conditions, reactions and the headline numbers.

Limestone is burnt with coke in shaft kilns; brine is ammoniated and carbonated with the kiln gas so sodium bicarbonate crystallises. The bicarbonate is filtered and calcined with steam to light soda ash, and the mother liquor is limed and steam stripped to recover the ammonia, leaving calcium chloride liquor as the effluent.
