Overview

Route, conditions, reactions and the headline numbers.

Refined vegetable oil reacts with methanol over a sodium hydroxide catalyst at about 60 C. The heavy glycerol layer settles out, the methanol is stripped and recycled, and the esters are washed and dried to fuel grade biodiesel. Crude glycerol is sold.
