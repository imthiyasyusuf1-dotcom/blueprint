Overview

Route, conditions, reactions and the headline numbers.

Ethane is cracked with dilution steam in fired tubular furnaces. The hot gas raises high pressure steam, is quenched with water, compressed to about 36 bar and dried, then chilled in a cold box. Columns separate hydrogen and methane (burnt as furnace fuel), ethylene, recycle ethane, propylene and a heavier cut.
