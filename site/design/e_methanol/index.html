Overview

Route, conditions, reactions and the headline numbers.

Electrolytic hydrogen and captured CO2 are compressed to loop pressure and converted on a copper zinc oxide catalyst in a boiling water reactor (CO2 + 3 H2 -> CH3OH + H2O). Unconverted gas is recycled, a small purge is burnt in the package boiler, and the crude methanol water mix is let down and distilled to AA grade.
