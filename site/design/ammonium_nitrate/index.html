Overview

Route, conditions, reactions and the headline numbers.

Ammonia gas and 60% nitric acid are neutralised at about 4 bar; the reaction heat boils off water and the process vapour raises clean low-pressure steam. The solution is concentrated to 96% in a vacuum evaporator and to a 99.7% melt, then prilled against air and cooled in a fluid bed.
