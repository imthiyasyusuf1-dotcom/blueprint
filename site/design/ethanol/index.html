Overview

Route, conditions, reactions and the headline numbers.

Dry grind: corn is milled, cooked with enzymes to turn starch into sugar, fermented by yeast, then distilled and dried on molecular sieves to fuel ethanol. The leftover grain is dried to DDGS for animal feed.
