Overview

Route, conditions, reactions and the headline numbers.

Benzene is alkylated with propylene over a zeolite to cumene. Cumene is oxidised with air to cumene hydroperoxide, which is concentrated and split with a trace of sulphuric acid into phenol and acetone. Both are purified by distillation; about 0.6 t of acetone comes with every tonne of phenol.
