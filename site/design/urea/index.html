Overview

Route, conditions, reactions and the headline numbers.

Liquid ammonia and compressed CO2 react at about 140 bar to ammonium carbamate, which dehydrates to urea. A steam heated stripper uses the fresh CO2 to strip unreacted carbamate back to the condenser, whose heat raises LP steam. A 4 bar decomposer, vacuum evaporators and a prill tower give solid urea.
