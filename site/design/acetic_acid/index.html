Overview

Route, conditions, reactions and the headline numbers.

Methanol and carbon monoxide react over an iridium iodide catalyst dissolved in acetic acid. The reactor liquid is flashed, the catalyst returns to the reactor, and the crude acid is dried and freed of heavy ends in three columns. The shift reaction and the vent are the only real losses.
