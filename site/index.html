    What you get

    Eight sections for every design, worked out from first principles and costed with a recognised textbook method. Change the inputs and the engine solves again in your browser.

    01

    Flow diagram

    Every unit and stream, with temperature, pressure and composition on tap.

    02

    Mass and energy balance

    Full stream table, closure and atom balance errors, utilities and their annual cost.

    03

    Kit list

    Each item sized and costed: type, size, material, pressure, purchased and bare module cost.

    04

    Capex and opex

    Fixed capital, cost of manufacture, NPV, IRR, payback and levelised cost per tonne.

    05

    Hazards

    GHS statements per substance, flammability limits, process hazard flags and a starter HAZOP.

    06

    Carbon

    Scope 1 and 2 emissions per tonne of product, plus the greener route worth looking at.

    07

    Validation

    Model results set against published plant figures, with the source and the error shown.

    08

    PDF report

    The whole design as a tidy report you can share, for the default case or your own inputs.
