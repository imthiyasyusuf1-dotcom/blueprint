About Blueprint

Founded and built by Yusuf Imthiyas.

What it is

Blueprint is a free web tool. You type a chemical and it returns a first pass design for a plant that makes it: a flow diagram, a solved mass and energy balance, a sized and costed kit list, capital and operating cost, hazards, carbon footprint and a PDF report.

Every number comes from a process model that solves the chemistry and the energy balance, a recognised costing method and sourced prices. Each design is then checked against published figures for real plants, and the result of that check is shown next to the design rather than hidden.

Who it is for

  • Students working on a design project who want a worked reference to check their own numbers against.
  • Early stage founders who need a sense of the size, cost and footprint of a plant before spending on a feasibility study.
  • Engineers scoping ideas who want a quick, transparent starting point to change and argue with.

What it is not

Blueprint is a screening tool. Its estimates are factored, typically within minus 30 to plus 50 percent, and the models are simplified on purpose. It is not a substitute for detailed engineering, a licensor package or professional advice, and nothing here is fit for construction.
