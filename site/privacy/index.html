Privacy

Privacy

Short version: no cookies, no analytics, and only what you choose to send.

What is collected

Nothing, unless you fill in a form. The waitlist form collects your email address. The request form collects your email address, the chemical, and optionally a capacity and a note.

How it is sent

Form entries are sent through FormSubmit, which forwards them by email to the founder. Blueprint keeps no database of its own.

How it is used

Only to reply to you and to announce new processes. Your details are never sold or shared. To be removed, reply to any email from Blueprint and ask.

Cookies and tracking

Blueprint sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Third party code

Pages load nothing from third parties. When you run a custom design, your browser downloads Python (Pyodide) from the jsDelivr CDN, and fpdf2 from the Python Package Index if you build a custom PDF. The design itself is solved on your device and is not sent anywhere.
