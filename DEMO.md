# 60 second demo script

For a screen recording on the phone (or laptop) and for showing it live.

0 to 5 s. Open the site. "This is Blueprint. You type a chemical and it designs the plant."

5 to 12 s. Type "ammonia", tap it. "Haber-Bosch, 2,000 tonnes a day. Done."

12 to 22 s. Scroll the flow diagram, tap a stream. "Every stream is solved: flows, temperatures,
pressures. The mass and atom balance closes, and it shows you by how much."

22 to 32 s. Kit list, then Costs. "Each piece of kit is sized from the balance and costed with
Turton correlations at today's prices. Capex, opex, cost per tonne, NPV, payback."

32 to 40 s. Validation tab. "And it checks itself against real published plants. Ammonia gas use
is within 2 percent of the EU benchmark, CO2 per tonne within 1 percent of IPCC."

40 to 48 s. Change capacity to 1,500 t/d, region to UK, hit run. "Change anything and it re-runs
the whole design in your browser in a few seconds."

48 to 55 s. Download the PDF. "Fifteen page report, ready to send."

55 to 60 s. "Eleven processes live, all free. Built by me. Link below."
